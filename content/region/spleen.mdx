The spleen filters blood and stages immune responses; the pancreas releases digestive enzymes and, from its islets, insulin and glucagon. Chinese medicine bundles them as one functional organ, "the Spleen".

The [[wp:Spleen|spleen]] filters blood and houses immune cells on the subject's left; the [[wp:Pancreas|pancreas]] behind the stomach handles digestion and blood sugar. The cookbook gives them one room because [[chinese-medicine|Chinese medicine]] does.

## What people talk about here

Overthinking and rumination are spleen weather in the old texts, which is why [[depression]] and [[autism|autistic]] looping threads land here. Blood sugar and the [[intestines|inflammatory-food]] conversation. Immune fatigue with [[cfs|ME / CFS]] and [[cancer]]. Chronic worry as an earth imbalance.

## Body

Red pulp recycles old red cells, white pulp holds lymphocytes; splenectomy raises infection risk. Pancreas: exocrine enzymes and endocrine islets — the blood-sugar thermostat.

## Tradition

Chinese medicine: the Spleen transforms and transports food into qi and blood, holds organs and blood in place, governs muscles and thought (yi); dampness and worry injure it (Earth). Human Design's Spleen center is a different thing: the immune-intuitive awareness center, spontaneous and in-the-moment.

## Practice

Warm, cooked, regular meals and moderate exercise are the TCM Spleen prescription; SP6 (Sanyinjiao) is its classic point; blood-sugar stability is the pancreatic one.
