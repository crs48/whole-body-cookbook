The largest internal organ (\~1.5 kg) and the body's chemical plant: it filters blood from the gut, makes bile, stores glycogen, builds proteins and clears hormones and toxins. It regenerates.

The [[wp:Liver|liver]] is the large organ under the right ribs, with the [[wp:Gallbladder|gallbladder]] tucked beneath it. It metabolises, stores, and clears. In [[chinese-medicine|Chinese medicine]] it is wood: the general who plans, and who seizes up when plans are blocked.

## What people talk about here

Liver qi stagnation is the phrase people reach for when [[depression]] feels like a jam rather than a hole. Irritability and [[adhd|ADHD]] frustration land here through the gallbladder pairing. Medication load, alcohol, and the v0.1 board's binder protocol are pinned here as author notes, not advice.

## In the atlas

- Meridians: the [[liver-meridian]] runs from the big toe up the inside of the [[left-leg|leg]] to the ribs; the [[gallbladder-meridian]] zigzags down the side of the body to the fourth toe. Both are wood.
- Structure: digestive tint.
- Folk: Jupiter in [[astrology]]; wood and spring in the five phases.

Related rooms: [[stomach]], [[spleen]], [[intestines]], [[solar-plexus]].

## Body

\~500 functions; hepatic portal system; Kupffer cells (immune); bile stored in the gallbladder and released on fat intake. Liver clears cortisol and oestrogen, so hormonal and metabolic complaints often route here.

## Tradition

Chinese medicine: the Liver stores blood, ensures the smooth flow of qi, governs tendons and eyes, houses the hun (ethereal soul); anger and frustration are its emotion, spring its season (Wood). Ayurveda: pitta organ, seat of rakta dhatu. Jupiter rules the liver (Sagittarius).

## Practice

Alcohol, fructose and drug load are the modern liver stressors; LV3 (Taichong) is the go-to point for stagnation; bitter foods stimulate bile.
