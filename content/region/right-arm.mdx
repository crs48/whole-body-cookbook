Shoulder, upper arm, elbow, forearm, wrist. The arm is the body's reach: six of the twelve meridians run its length, and it carries the brachial plexus from the neck to the hand.

The right [[wp:Arm|arm]] sits at negative X in the body frame, the viewer's left on the plate. It is the dominant arm for most people and the one the making and stimming threads mention first.

## What people talk about here

Repetitive strain, tennis elbow, and the making conversation from artists improving dexterity. Shoulder tension from [[anxiety]]. [[mobility]] threads about crutches and cane hands.

## In the atlas

- Meridians: the [[large-intestine-meridian]], [[triple-warmer-meridian]], and [[small-intestine-meridian]] all start at the fingers and run up the outside of this arm to the face. The atlas draws them here for legibility; the body has them on both arms.
- Structure: skeleton and muscle tints.
- Folk: Gemini in [[astrology]].

Related rooms: [[hands]], [[left-arm]], [[face]], [[throat]].

## Body

Humerus, radius, ulna; brachial plexus (C5–T1) → median, ulnar, radial nerves. Shoulder mobility depends on the scapula gliding on the ribcage.

## Tradition

Arm meridians: Lung, Heart and Pericardium (yin, inside), Large Intestine, Small Intestine and Triple Warmer (yang, outside). Gemini rules arms and shoulders. In Anatomy Trains the four arm lines continue the trunk lines into the hand.

## Practice

Shoulder and neck tension commonly reads as arm symptoms; nerve-glide exercises; the arms are the most accessible acupuncture territory.
