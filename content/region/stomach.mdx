A muscular bag that stores, churns and acidifies food (pH \~1.5–3.5), begins protein digestion and meters chyme into the small intestine. Its mucosa and its nerves make it a mood organ as much as a food organ.

The [[wp:Stomach|stomach]] sits on the subject's left under the ribs. It is the first stop of digestion and, in most traditions, the organ of worry.

## What people talk about here

Nausea with [[anxiety]] and with medication. Appetite that vanishes with [[depression]] or arrives sideways with [[adhd|ADHD]] stimulants. Reflux and the [[intestines|inflammatory-food]] conversation from the v0.1 board. Chinese medicine pairs the stomach with the [[spleen]] as the earth pair and ties both to rumination.

## Body

Parietal cells (acid, intrinsic factor), G cells (gastrin), ghrelin — the hunger hormone — is made here. Vagal fibres govern secretion and motility; stress reliably alters both.

## Tradition

Chinese medicine: the Stomach "rots and ripens" food and, with the Spleen, is the root of post-natal qi (Earth element; worry and overthinking injure it). Ayurveda: seat of agni, the digestive fire, and pitta. Moon rules the stomach and breasts (Cancer).

## Practice

Meal rhythm, chewing, warm food in Ayurveda and TCM; acupoint ST36 (Zusanli) is the classic tonifying point; the gut–brain axis makes stress work digestive work.
