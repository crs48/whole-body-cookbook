Shoulder, upper arm, elbow, forearm, wrist. The arm is the body's reach: six of the twelve meridians run its length, and it carries the brachial plexus from the neck to the hand.

The left [[wp:Arm|arm]] sits at positive X in the body frame, which is the viewer's right on the anterior plate. Shoulder, elbow, and wrist landmarks carry the channels; the [[hands]] get their own room.

## What people talk about here

Shoulder and neck tension that travels down. Heart-side sensations people notice before they notice the [[heart]]. Repetitive strain from the [[hands]]. [[eds|EDS]] elbows and shoulders.

## In the atlas

- Meridians: the [[lung-meridian]], [[pericardium-meridian]], and [[heart-meridian]] all run down the inside of this arm to the thumb, middle finger, and little finger. The atlas draws them here for legibility; the body has them on both arms.
- Structure: skeleton, muscle, and cardiovascular tints.
- Folk: Gemini rules the arms in [[astrology]].

Related rooms: [[hands]], [[right-arm]], [[heart]], [[lungs]].

## Body

Humerus, radius, ulna; brachial plexus (C5–T1) → median, ulnar, radial nerves. Shoulder mobility depends on the scapula gliding on the ribcage.

## Tradition

Arm meridians: Lung, Heart and Pericardium (yin, inside), Large Intestine, Small Intestine and Triple Warmer (yang, outside). Gemini rules arms and shoulders. In Anatomy Trains the four arm lines continue the trunk lines into the hand.

## Practice

Shoulder and neck tension commonly reads as arm symptoms; nerve-glide exercises; the arms are the most accessible acupuncture territory.
