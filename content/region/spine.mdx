33 vertebrae protecting the spinal cord — the highway between brain and body, and the axis every subtle system runs along: sushumna, Du mai, the HD channel column, the torus axis.

The [[wp:Vertebral column|spine]] runs from the base of the skull to the tailbone and carries the spinal cord. In the atlas it is the midline landmark chain: C7, T12, L4, sacrum. Every tradition draws its main channel along it.

## What people talk about here

Back pain and [[mobility]]: canes, chairs, stairs, and the social model. [[eds|EDS]] instability. The [[sympathetic]] chain that runs beside the vertebrae and shows up in [[pots|POTS]] and [[cptsd|CPTSD]] threads. Sitting practice and the move-daily recipe from the v0.1 board.

## In the atlas

- Nerves: the [[sympathetic]] chain, two ganglion cords either side of the column.
- Channels: the [[bladder-meridian]] runs down both sides of the back; the Du vessel runs up the midline; sushumna, ida, and pingala are the yogic reading of the same corridor.
- Centers: every chakra from [[muladhara]] to [[ajna]] is a station on this axis.
- Folk: Leo rules the back in [[astrology]]; the [[mystical|kundalini]] rises here.

Related rooms: [[pelvis]], [[crown]], [[kidneys]], [[autonomic]].

## Body

Cervical (7), thoracic (12), lumbar (5), sacrum, coccyx. Discs, facet joints, the cord and 31 pairs of spinal nerves; the sympathetic chain runs alongside. Dermatomes map skin to segments.

## Tradition

Sushumna nadi runs its length with ida and pingala coiling around it; kundalini is pictured rising from the sacrum. Du mai (Governing vessel) climbs the back. The djed pillar of Osiris, the Tree of Life's middle pillar, the world axis. Saturn rules the spine and bones (Leo the heart and back).

## Practice

Spinal mobility, yoga, Feldenkrais, osteopathy and chiropractic; spinal breathing (Taoist microcosmic orbit); posture as autonomic lever.
