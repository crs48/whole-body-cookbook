About 1.3–1.4 kg, \~86 billion neurons and as many glia, using roughly a fifth of the body's energy at rest. It constructs perception, predicts the body's needs, and holds the models we call self.

The [[wp:Human brain|brain]] is where the medical model keeps most of what this cookbook files under mind. Neurodivergence, mood, attention, sensory processing, and the [[vagus]] all have a room here, and the room is crowded.

## What people talk about here

[[adhd|ADHD]] and [[autism]] threads describe a nervous system with a different sampling rate rather than a broken one. [[depression]] shows up as circuitry, weather, and inflammation depending on who is talking. [[ocd|OCD]] is the loop. Brain fog is pinned here by [[cfs|ME / CFS]], [[long-covid|long COVID]], and [[pots|POTS]] people, usually with the note that it is not the same as being tired.

## Body

Cerebrum (cortex and deep nuclei), cerebellum, brainstem. The hypothalamus–pituitary axis makes the brain the master endocrine organ; the brainstem houses the autonomic and vagal nuclei that set the body's internal weather.

## Tradition

Chinese medicine calls the brain the "sea of marrow", nourished by Kidney essence; shen (spirit, mind) is housed in the Heart, not the brain. Ayurveda: seat of prana and the majja dhatu; the brow and crown chakras map onto pituitary and pineal in many modern syntheses.

## Evidence

Interoception (Craig, Barrett): insula and anterior cingulate integrate body signals into feeling; predictive-processing models treat emotion as the brain's best guess about the body. Neuroplasticity is robustly demonstrated; "left-brain/right-brain" personality is not.

## Practice

Sleep, movement, novelty and social contact are the best-evidenced brain care. Neurofeedback, meditation and breathwork measurably shift network activity.
