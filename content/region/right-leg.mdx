Femur, tibia, fibula — the longest bones — and the largest muscles. Six meridians run the leg; the sciatic nerve is the body's widest.

The right [[wp:Human leg|leg]] mirrors the [[left-leg]] at negative X. The yang meridians descend along it from the face and back to the toes.

## What people talk about here

The same [[mobility]], [[pots|POTS]], and [[eds|EDS]] threads as the other leg. Sciatica. Restless legs at night with [[adhd|ADHD]] and [[anxiety]].

## In the atlas

- Meridians: the [[stomach-meridian]] down the front, the [[gallbladder-meridian]] down the side, the [[bladder-meridian]] down the back. Drawn here for legibility; present on both legs.
- Nerves: the [[sciatic]] nerve.
- Structure: skeleton and muscle tints.
- Folk: Sagittarius and Capricorn in [[astrology]].

Related rooms: [[feet]], [[left-leg]], [[pelvis]], [[spine]].

## Body

Lumbosacral plexus → femoral, obturator, sciatic (tibial and peroneal). Standing is an active balancing act driven by calf muscle pumps that return blood to the heart — the mechanism that fails in orthostatic intolerance.

## Tradition

Leg meridians: Spleen, Liver and Kidney (yin, inside), Stomach, Gallbladder and Bladder (yang, outside/back). Sagittarius the thighs, Capricorn the knees, Aquarius the shins and ankles. Legs are the earth pole of the body in most chakra systems.

## Practice

Calf raises, compression and leg crossing counter blood pooling; ST36 below the knee is the most-used point in acupuncture; walking meditation.
