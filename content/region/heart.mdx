A \~300 g muscular pump beating \~100,000 times a day, with its own pacemaker, its own nervous system (the intrinsic cardiac ganglia) and the body's strongest rhythmic electromagnetic field. Across cultures it is the organ of feeling and of the self.

The [[wp:Heart|heart]] sits a little left of the midline behind the sternum, wrapped in the [[wp:Pericardium|pericardium]] and flanked by the [[lungs]]. In this cookbook it is the busiest room: the physical pump, the place people point to when they say grief, and the door that Chinese medicine, Human Design, and the chakra map all treat as central.

## What people talk about here

Standing heart rate that jumps when you get up is the signature of [[pots|POTS]]. Chest weather that will not settle is [[anxiety]] in the language of this map. A heavy, slow chest is where [[depression]] and grief overlap in the old texts. Palpitations, compression garments, salt, and the small ritual of lying down with legs up all get pinned here.

## In the atlas

- Meridians: the [[heart-meridian]] runs from the heart down the inside of the arm to the little finger; the [[pericardium-meridian]] runs beside it to the middle finger. Both are fire.
- Nerves: the [[vagus]] slows the heart; the [[sympathetic]] chain speeds it. Their argument is the [[autonomic]] field.
- Centers: [[anahata]] in the chakra map; the [[hd-g|G center]] and the [[hd-ego|heart / ego center]] in Human Design.
- Folk: Leo and the Sun in [[astrology]].

Related rooms: [[lungs]], [[solar-plexus]], [[spine]], [[autonomic]].

## Body

Four chambers, coronary arteries, the sinoatrial node. Heart-rate variability (HRV) reflects vagal tone and is one of the best non-invasive windows on autonomic state. The heart secretes hormones (ANP, BNP) and sends more signals to the brain than it receives.

## Tradition

Chinese medicine: the Heart houses shen (spirit-mind) and governs blood and vessels; the Emperor. Anahata, the heart chakra: love, compassion, the meeting of upper and lower. Sufi qalb, the Sacred Heart, the Egyptian weighing of the heart. Human Design's Heart/Ego center is willpower, not affection. Sun rules the heart.

## Evidence

HRV biofeedback and slow breathing (\~6/min) reliably improve vagal tone and mood. Claims about heart "intuition" or heart-field communication between people are not established.

## Practice

Coherence breathing, loving-kindness meditation, cardiovascular exercise; in dysautonomia, tracking HR on standing is a direct measurement of this room's regulation.
