27 bones, \~30 muscles, and a quarter of the motor cortex. Six meridians begin or end at the fingertips, and the hand is a reflex map of the whole body.

The [[wp:Hand|hands]] are the body's densest tool. In the atlas the fingertips reach toward `x = ±1`, the edge of the unit body, and six of the twelve regular meridians begin or end there.

## What people talk about here

Stimming and fidgeting from [[autism|autistic]] and [[adhd|ADHD]] people. Dexterity practice from the artists in the audience. Washing and checking in [[ocd|OCD]]. Cold hands in [[pots|POTS]]. The non-dominant-hand toothbrush drill from the v0.1 board. Phones, and the digital detox recipe.

## Body

Median, ulnar and radial nerves; the fingertips have the densest touch receptors in the body after the lips. Fine motor control and tool use shaped the human brain.

## Tradition

Meridian endpoints: LU11 thumb, LI1 index, PC9 middle, TW1 ring, HT9 / SI1 little finger. Palmar reflexology mirrors the body onto the palm. Mercury rules the hands. Mudras encode meaning in finger positions; palmistry reads character from the lines. Laogong (PC8) in the palm is the qigong emitting point.

## Practice

Hand reflexology, mudra, acupressure on the jing-well points, grip strength as a marker of general health.
