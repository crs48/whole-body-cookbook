26 bones and over 100 muscles, tendons and ligaments per foot, \~7,000 nerve endings each. The base of standing, the origin of three meridians and the end of three more, and the most-used reflexology map.

The [[wp:Foot|feet]] are the bottom of the unit body. Kidney-1, the bubbling spring point on the sole, is a landmark in the atlas and the lowest acupoint on the body.

## What people talk about here

Grounding practices, barefoot walking, and the move-daily recipe. Cold feet and pooling in [[pots|POTS]]. Ankles in [[eds|EDS]] and [[mobility]] threads. Reflexology maps, which the cookbook treats as another drawing of the same rooms.

## In the atlas

- Meridians: the [[kidney-meridian]] starts on the sole; the [[spleen-meridian]] and [[liver-meridian]] start at the big toe; the [[stomach-meridian]], [[gallbladder-meridian]], and [[bladder-meridian]] end at the second, fourth, and fifth toes.
- Field: the earth star sits below the soles at `y = -1.28`; the ground disc at `y = -1.4`.
- Folk: Pisces rules the feet in [[astrology]].

Related rooms: [[left-leg]], [[right-leg]], [[kidneys]], [[whole]].

## Body

Arches act as springs; proprioceptors in the sole feed balance; foot problems propagate up the whole kinetic chain.

## Tradition

KI1 "Bubbling Spring" is where Kidney qi rises from the earth; BL67 at the little toe, SP1 / LR1 at the big toe. Plantar reflexology maps the body head-to-toe from toes to heel. The earth star chakra hangs below the feet. Pisces rules the feet.

## Practice

Barefoot time, foot reflexology, grounding/earthing practices, foot soaks in Chinese medicine.
