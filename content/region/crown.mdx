The vertex of the skull, where the sagittal and coronal sutures meet (bregma, the soft anterior fontanelle in infants). In almost every contemplative tradition it is the door: where the self meets what is larger than the self.

The crown is the [[wp:Vertex (anatomy)|vertex]] of the skull and everything the traditions hang above it. Anatomically it is scalp, bone, and the top of the [[brain]]. In the atlas it is the landmark at `y = 1`, the unit of the whole body frame.

## What people talk about here

Attention, pressure, the feeling of a lid. [[adhd|ADHD]] threads pin here when the topic is focus rather than movement. Headache and migraine notes land here or on the [[face]]. Meditators describe tingling or opening at the crown; the cookbook records the description and does not grade it.

## In the atlas

- Centers: [[sahasrara]] in the chakra map; the [[hd-head|head center]] in Human Design, which holds gates 64, 61, and 63.
- Channels: the [[bladder-meridian]] crosses the crown on its way from the inner eye to the back; the Du vessel ends near the nose after running over the top of the head.
- Field: the soul star sits above the crown at `y = 1.28`, the first bead outside the flesh.
- Folk: Aries rules the head in [[astrology]]; the crown is the upper end of the [[mystical|kundalini corridor]].

Related rooms: [[brain]], [[face]], [[spine]], [[whole]].

## Body

Bone (parietal), scalp, the superior sagittal sinus draining venous blood just beneath. Du 20 (Baihui, "hundred meetings") sits here in acupuncture.

## Tradition

Sahasrara, the thousand-petalled lotus; the Taoist Baihui; the Sikh dasam duar (tenth gate); the Tibetan aperture of Brahma used in phowa practice at death. Human Design places the Head center (pressure to know) here.

## Practice

Crown-focused meditation, light visualisation, and the acupuncture point Baihui for lifting qi, clarity and prolapse.
