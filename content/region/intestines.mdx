About 7 m of small intestine (absorption) and 1.5 m of colon (water, fermentation, microbiome), lined by the largest immune tissue in the body and wired by the enteric nervous system — \~500 million neurons, the "second brain".

The small and large [[wp:Gastrointestinal tract|intestines]] fill the belly below the [[stomach]]. They hold the [[wp:Gut microbiota|microbiome]] and the [[enteric]] nervous system, which is why the gut is called the second brain and why so many mood threads end up here.

## What people talk about here

Gut and mood: [[depression]] and [[anxiety]] people trade notes on inflammation, fibre, and elimination. [[autism|Autistic]] and [[adhd|ADHD]] threads about interoception and food texture. IBS, [[eds|EDS]] gut motility, and the very old link between the bowel and the [[skin]].

<Protocol>
Cut down inflammatory food (sugars, starches, many oils; coconut and greens as a counter-weight), learn to poop, and binders so what the tissues dump does not reabsorb. These are the author's practices from the v0.1 board, not prescriptions.
</Protocol>

## Body

Villi and microvilli give \~30 m² of absorptive surface. The microbiome (\~38 trillion bacteria) makes vitamins, short-chain fatty acids and most of the body's serotonin precursors. The gut talks to the brain through the vagus, immune signals and hormones.

## Tradition

Chinese medicine: Small Intestine separates pure from impure (paired with Heart); Large Intestine lets go (paired with Lung; grief). The lower dantien and hara sit here — the Japanese seat of self and courage. Svadhisthana overlaps the lower gut. Virgo rules the intestines.

## Evidence

The gut–brain axis is real and bidirectional; microbiome effects on mood in humans are promising but early. Interoceptive sensitivity to the gut varies enormously between people.

## Practice

Fibre diversity, fermented foods, meal timing; gut-directed hypnotherapy has strong evidence for IBS; abdominal breathing and hara work in martial arts and Zen.
