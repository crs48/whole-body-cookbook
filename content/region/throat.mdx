The narrow pass where air, food, voice and the great vessels to the head all share a few centimetres. Voice, swallowing and the thyroid's metabolic thermostat live here.

The [[wp:Throat|throat]] carries air, food, voice, and the [[wp:Thyroid|thyroid]]. It is a corridor between the [[brain]] and the chest, and the [[vagus]] runs right through it.

## What people talk about here

Lump-in-the-throat feelings with [[anxiety]] and grief. Voice that goes when a person is overwhelmed. Thyroid threads that overlap with [[depression]] and [[cfs|fatigue]]. Swallowing and reflux notes from the [[intestines|inflammatory-food]] conversation. Hoarseness after [[long-covid|COVID]].

## Body

Larynx and vocal folds (vagus, via the recurrent laryngeal nerve), thyroid and parathyroids, carotid bodies sensing blood oxygen, the pharyngeal plexus. Swallowing needs six cranial nerves.

## Tradition

Vishuddha, the throat chakra: expression, truth, purification. Human Design: the Throat center, where everything becomes manifest as word or deed. Chinese medicine: Ren 22–23, the Lung and Stomach channels pass here. Venus rules the throat and neck (Taurus).

## Practice

Chanting, humming and toning stimulate the vagus and vocal apparatus; speech and voice therapy; thyroid testing when energy, temperature and mood drift together.
