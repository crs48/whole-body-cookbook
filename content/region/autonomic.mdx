The involuntary control system: sympathetic (mobilise), parasympathetic (rest, digest, connect) and enteric branches. It sets heart rate, blood pressure, digestion, pupil size, temperature — the body's weather.

The [[wp:Autonomic nervous system|autonomic nervous system]] runs heart rate, blood pressure, digestion, sweat, and pupils without permission. It has no single location, so the atlas draws it as a dashed cloud in the field family and gives it a room.

## What people talk about here

[[pots|POTS]] and dysautonomia: the standing problem. Freeze and shutdown in [[cptsd|CPTSD]] and [[autism]]. [[cfs|ME / CFS]] crashes and [[long-covid|long COVID]]. The slow-down practice from the v0.1 board, which is mostly a practice of letting this system stop bracing. Ninety-second feelings.

## Body

Sympathetic chain ganglia beside the spine (T1–L2); parasympathetic via cranial nerves (mainly vagus) and S2–S4. Dysautonomia (e.g. POTS) is when this system fails to hold blood pressure and heart rate steady on standing.

## Tradition

Polyvagal theory (Porges) reframes the ANS as a hierarchy of safety states — ventral vagal (social), sympathetic (fight/flight), dorsal vagal (shutdown); influential in trauma therapy though contested in neuroscience. Traditional systems read the same terrain as yin/yang balance or vata disturbance.

## Practice

Breathwork, cold exposure, vagal stimulation, orthostatic training, salt and fluids, compression; nervous-system regulation as the shared vocabulary of somatic therapy.
