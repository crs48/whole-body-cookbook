The celiac plexus — a dense knot of sympathetic ganglia around the aorta that innervates most of the abdominal organs. Anatomically a nerve centre, experientially the place where fear and excitement are felt.

The [[wp:Celiac plexus|celiac plexus]] is a real bundle of nerve tissue behind the [[stomach]]. Folk use has widened it into the whole upper belly, the place a punch lands and where a decision is felt before it is thought.

## What people talk about here

Gut-level dread with [[anxiety]] and [[cptsd|CPTSD]]. The [[adhd|ADHD]] experience of interest as a physical tug. The trust-yourself practice from the v0.1 board points here: the body's own instruments live in this room. [[ocd|OCD]] doubt is often described as a stomach-level wrongness.

## Body

Celiac ganglia, the greater splanchnic nerves and vagal branches; supplies stomach, liver, spleen, pancreas, kidneys, adrenals and gut. A blow here provokes the "winded" spasm.

## Tradition

Manipura, the navel/solar-plexus chakra: fire, will, digestion, personal power. Human Design's Solar Plexus center is the emotional wave and, when defined, emotional Authority. Chinese medicine: Ren 12 (Zhongwan) and the Middle Burner.
