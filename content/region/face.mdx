The body's display and intake surface — expression, speech, breath, food — and in several traditions a diagnostic mirror of the organs.

The [[wp:Face|face]] is the body's public surface and its densest sensory patch. Eyes, nose, mouth, jaw, and the [[wp:Paranasal sinuses|sinuses]] all live here, along with everything a face is asked to perform.

## What people talk about here

[[autism|Autistic]] people pin masking here: the labour of arranging a face for other people. Jaw clenching and TMJ notes arrive with [[anxiety]]. Sinus pressure, post-nasal drip, and food reactions land here when the topic is [[intestines|diet]] rather than allergy. The v0.1 board's positive self-talk drill happens in the mirror, left eye first.

## In the atlas

- Meridians: the [[stomach-meridian]] starts under the eye; the [[large-intestine-meridian]] ends beside the nose; the [[gallbladder-meridian]] starts at the outer eye; the [[triple-warmer-meridian]] ends at the eyebrow; the [[bladder-meridian]] starts at the inner eye.
- Nerves: the [[vagus]] is close by in the [[throat]]; the [[sympathetic]] chain reaches the eye.
- Folk: Taurus for the neck and jaw in [[astrology]]; the face reads the [[skin]] before the mind does.

Related rooms: [[brain]], [[throat]], [[crown]], [[skin]].

## Body

Facial nerve (VII) drives expression, trigeminal (V) carries sensation and moves the jaw. The face is densely mapped in the sensory cortex. Cranial nerves make it the most direct external window onto the brainstem.

## Tradition

Mien shiang (Chinese face reading) maps organs onto zones: forehead–bladder/intestines, between the brows–liver, nose–heart/spleen, cheeks–lungs, chin–kidneys and hormones. Mars rules the face and head in medical astrology (Aries).

## Practice

Facial acupuncture and gua sha; facial expression as a lever on state (the face–vagus link in polyvagal theory); masking as a lived, effortful practice for many neurodivergent people.
