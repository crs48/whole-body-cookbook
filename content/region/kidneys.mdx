Two fist-sized filters passing the whole blood volume \~40 times a day, balancing water, salts, acid and blood pressure. Above them the adrenals make cortisol, aldosterone and adrenaline — the stress hormones.

Two [[wp:Kidney|kidneys]] sit against the back wall of the belly with the [[wp:Adrenal gland|adrenals]] on top. They filter blood and balance water and salt. In [[chinese-medicine|Chinese medicine]] they store jing, the inherited reserve, and their emotion is fear.

## What people talk about here

Salt and fluid loading in [[pots|POTS]] recipes. Adrenal exhaustion as a folk description of [[cfs|ME / CFS]] and burnout. Kidney water versus heart fire as one reading of [[anxiety]]. Fear that lives in the low back.

## Body

\~1 million nephrons each; erythropoietin (red cell production) and active vitamin D come from the kidney. Adrenal cortex: cortisol and aldosterone; medulla: adrenaline. The HPA axis ends here.

## Tradition

Chinese medicine: the Kidney stores jing (essence), rules bones, marrow, ears, hair and reproduction, and is the root of yin and yang — fear is its emotion, winter its season (Water). Ayurveda: apana vayu and the pelvic bowl. Venus/Libra rule the kidneys and lower back.

## Practice

Hydration and salt in dysautonomia; KI1 (Yongquan) on the sole and KI3 (Taixi) at the ankle are the great grounding and tonifying points; adrenal "fatigue" is not a medical diagnosis, HPA-axis dysregulation is a research topic.
