## Body

Three layers (epidermis, dermis, hypodermis), millions of mechanoreceptors and free nerve endings, vitamin D synthesis, sweat and sebum. Fascia is richly innervated and contains contractile cells; it transmits force between muscle groups.

## Tradition

Chinese medicine: the skin is the domain of the Lung and of wei qi, the defensive layer; sweating regulates it. Ayurveda: skin reflects rasa and rakta dhatu and the doshas (dry–vata, red/hot–pitta, oily–kapha). Astrology: Saturn rules skin, bones and boundaries.

## Evidence

Fascia research (Fascia Research Congress, Schleip, Stecco) supports fascia as a sensory organ and force-transmitting network; Anatomy Trains lines are a useful model, partly supported by dissection studies.
