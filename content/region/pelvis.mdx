The bony basin carrying the trunk onto the legs and holding bladder, bowel and reproductive organs; the pelvic floor is the muscular hammock at its base. Its centre point is the atlas origin.

The [[wp:Pelvis|pelvis]] is the bowl of bone between the [[spine]] and the legs. The atlas puts its origin here, at the perineum, because everything above and below is measured from it.

## What people talk about here

Hip pain and [[mobility]] aids. Pelvic floor, bladder, and reproductive threads. [[eds|EDS]] subluxation. Sexuality and shame in the [[cptsd|CPTSD]] conversation. The lower dantien in qigong; the root and sacral rooms in every chakra thread.

## In the atlas

- Nerves: the [[sciatic]] nerve leaves the pelvis and runs down each leg.
- Centers: [[muladhara]] and [[svadhisthana]] in the chakra map; the [[hd-root|root]] and [[hd-sacral|sacral]] centers in Human Design.
- Meridians: the [[kidney-meridian]] passes the pubic bone; the [[bladder-meridian]] crosses the sacrum. Ren and Du vessels both begin at the perineum.
- Folk: Scorpio in [[astrology]]; the lower dantien in [[chinese-medicine|Chinese medicine]].

Related rooms: [[spine]], [[intestines]], [[left-leg]], [[right-leg]].

## Body

Ilium, ischium, pubis, sacrum. Pelvic floor (levator ani, coccygeus) controls continence, supports organs, and co-contracts with the diaphragm in breathing. Sacral parasympathetic nerves (S2–S4) govern bladder, bowel and sexual function.

## Tradition

Muladhara (root chakra) sits at the perineum: earth, survival, safety. Ren 1 (Huiyin) is here — the meeting of yin. The lower dantien, the hara, the Root center in Human Design (pressure, adrenaline). Scorpio rules pelvis and genitals.

## Practice

Pelvic-floor physiotherapy, mula bandha, Kegels and their release; grounding practices literally anchor at this point.
