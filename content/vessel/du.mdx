The "sea of yang channels", running up the back. With Ren mai it forms the microcosmic orbit of Taoist practice.

## Tradition

Governs all yang; treats spine, brain and back. Du 4 (Mingmen, "gate of life") between the kidneys; Du 14 at C7; Du 20 at the crown; Du 26 under the nose revives consciousness.

## Practice

Microcosmic orbit: breathe qi up the Du on the inhale and down the Ren on the exhale.
