The "sea of yin channels", running up the front; governs reproduction, the abdomen and chest.

## Tradition

Ren 4 and Ren 6 below the navel tonify essence and qi; Ren 12 the stomach; Ren 17 (Shanzhong) the chest and emotions. The dantien lie along it.

## Practice

Abdominal breathing and dantien work happen on this line.
