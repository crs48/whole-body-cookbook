Pleasure, emotion, sexuality, creativity, flow. "One's own abode."

[[wp:Svadhisthana|Svadhisthana]] is one of the seven chakras of the yogic subtle body. The atlas draws it as a disk on the midline: 0.4 of the way from the pubic symphysis to the navel. The formula is an offset along landmarks, so the same point works on a mesh later.

## What people pin

Sexuality and shame. Creative energy and burnout. The lower belly and [[intestines|gut]]. The same room as the [[hd-sacral|HD sacral center]].

## In the atlas

- Family: centers, group chakras. Turning the chakras on turns the [[human-design|Human Design centers]] off; the two maps never sit at full opacity together.
- Rooms: [[pelvis]], [[intestines]], the lower dantien.
- See [[mystical|mystical traditions]] for the map as a whole.

## Tradition

Element water; seed syllable VAM. In the Shat-chakra-nirupana (1577) and its Tantric sources, the chakras are meditation supports along sushumna — visualised, not assumed to be anatomical.

## Body

Modern syntheses pair it with the gonads and the pelvic organs. The mapping is 20th-century (Leadbeater, Woodroffe), not classical.

## Symbol

Balanced: feeling and flow. Excess: overwhelm, addiction. Deficient: numbness, rigidity.

## Practice

Breath, seed-sound chanting, colour visualisation, asana focused on the region; in trauma-informed work, chakras function as a somatic vocabulary for where feeling lives.
