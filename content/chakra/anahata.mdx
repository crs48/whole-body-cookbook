Love, compassion, grief, the bridge between the three lower and three upper centres. "Unstruck" — the sound not made by two things striking.

[[wp:Anahata|Anahata]] is one of the seven chakras of the yogic subtle body. The atlas draws it as a disk on the midline: 0.55 of the way from the xiphoid to the sternal notch. The formula is an offset along landmarks, so the same point works on a mesh later.

## What people pin

Grief and opening. Breath practice. The [[heart]] as the seat of both love and [[pots|POTS]]. Heart-centred prayer in the hesychast and Sufi maps.

## In the atlas

- Family: centers, group chakras. Turning the chakras on turns the [[human-design|Human Design centers]] off; the two maps never sit at full opacity together.
- Rooms: [[heart]], [[lungs]], the [[hd-g|HD G center]].
- See [[mystical|mystical traditions]] for the map as a whole.

## Tradition

Element air; seed syllable YAM. In the Shat-chakra-nirupana (1577) and its Tantric sources, the chakras are meditation supports along sushumna — visualised, not assumed to be anatomical.

## Body

Modern syntheses pair it with the thymus and heart. The mapping is 20th-century (Leadbeater, Woodroffe), not classical.

## Symbol

Balanced: warmth and boundaries together. Excess: codependence. Deficient: isolation, guardedness.

## Practice

Breath, seed-sound chanting, colour visualisation, asana focused on the region; in trauma-informed work, chakras function as a somatic vocabulary for where feeling lives.
