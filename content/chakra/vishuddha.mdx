Expression, truth, listening, purification. "Especially pure."

[[wp:Vishuddha|Vishuddha]] is one of the seven chakras of the yogic subtle body. The atlas draws it as a disk on the midline: at the larynx. The formula is an offset along landmarks, so the same point works on a mesh later.

## What people pin

Voice that goes under stress. Saying the thing. Swallowing and the [[throat]]. The [[hd-throat|HD throat center]] occupies the same landmark.

## In the atlas

- Family: centers, group chakras. Turning the chakras on turns the [[human-design|Human Design centers]] off; the two maps never sit at full opacity together.
- Rooms: [[throat]], the [[vagus]].
- See [[mystical|mystical traditions]] for the map as a whole.

## Tradition

Element space / ether; seed syllable HAM. In the Shat-chakra-nirupana (1577) and its Tantric sources, the chakras are meditation supports along sushumna — visualised, not assumed to be anatomical.

## Body

Modern syntheses pair it with the thyroid. The mapping is 20th-century (Leadbeater, Woodroffe), not classical.

## Symbol

Balanced: clear voice, honest speech. Excess: talking over. Deficient: swallowing words.

## Practice

Breath, seed-sound chanting, colour visualisation, asana focused on the region; in trauma-informed work, chakras function as a somatic vocabulary for where feeling lives.
