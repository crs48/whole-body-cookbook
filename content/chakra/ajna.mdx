Insight, imagination, intuition, seeing pattern. "Command."

[[wp:Ajna|Ajna]] is one of the seven chakras of the yogic subtle body. The atlas draws it as a disk on the midline: at the glabella, between the brows. The formula is an offset along landmarks, so the same point works on a mesh later.

## What people pin

Focus and attention in [[adhd|ADHD]]. Headache and pressure between the eyes. Visualisation practice. The [[hd-ajna|HD ajna center]] shares the landmark.

## In the atlas

- Family: centers, group chakras. Turning the chakras on turns the [[human-design|Human Design centers]] off; the two maps never sit at full opacity together.
- Rooms: [[brain]], [[face]], the endocrine nodes for pineal and pituitary.
- See [[mystical|mystical traditions]] for the map as a whole.

## Tradition

Element light / mind; seed syllable OM. In the Shat-chakra-nirupana (1577) and its Tantric sources, the chakras are meditation supports along sushumna — visualised, not assumed to be anatomical.

## Body

Modern syntheses pair it with the pituitary (or pineal). The mapping is 20th-century (Leadbeater, Woodroffe), not classical.

## Symbol

Balanced: clarity. Excess: fantasy, headaches. Deficient: denial, poor memory.

## Practice

Breath, seed-sound chanting, colour visualisation, asana focused on the region; in trauma-informed work, chakras function as a somatic vocabulary for where feeling lives.
