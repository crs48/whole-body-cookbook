Survival, safety, belonging, the body's right to exist. Kundalini is pictured coiled here.

[[wp:Muladhara|Muladhara]] is one of the seven chakras of the yogic subtle body. The atlas draws it as a disk on the midline: at the perineum, the origin of the body frame. The formula is an offset along landmarks, so the same point works on a mesh later.

## What people pin

Grounding and safety in [[cptsd|CPTSD]] threads. The [[pelvis]] and the legs. Fear as a root problem in the folk map, which overlaps the kidney reading in [[chinese-medicine|Chinese medicine]].

## In the atlas

- Family: centers, group chakras. Turning the chakras on turns the [[human-design|Human Design centers]] off; the two maps never sit at full opacity together.
- Rooms: [[pelvis]], the [[hd-root|HD root center]], the earth star below the [[feet]].
- See [[mystical|mystical traditions]] for the map as a whole.

## Tradition

Element earth; seed syllable LAM. In the Shat-chakra-nirupana (1577) and its Tantric sources, the chakras are meditation supports along sushumna — visualised, not assumed to be anatomical.

## Body

Modern syntheses pair it with the adrenals and pelvic floor. The mapping is 20th-century (Leadbeater, Woodroffe), not classical.

## Symbol

Balanced: grounded, present. Excess: rigidity, hoarding. Deficient: fear, dissociation from the body.

## Practice

Breath, seed-sound chanting, colour visualisation, asana focused on the region; in trauma-informed work, chakras function as a somatic vocabulary for where feeling lives.
