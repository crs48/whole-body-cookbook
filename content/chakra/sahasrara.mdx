Unity, transcendence, the dissolution of the separate self. Strictly, above the chakra system rather than the seventh of it.

[[wp:Sahasrara|Sahasrara]] is one of the seven chakras of the yogic subtle body. The atlas draws it as a disk on the midline: at the crown, y = 1, the top of the unit body. The formula is an offset along landmarks, so the same point works on a mesh later.

## What people pin

Meditation experiences at the [[crown]]. Kundalini arriving. The feel-as-good-as-you-want recipe. The [[hd-head|HD head center]] sits just below it.

## In the atlas

- Family: centers, group chakras. Turning the chakras on turns the [[human-design|Human Design centers]] off; the two maps never sit at full opacity together.
- Rooms: [[crown]], the soul star, the [[mystical|kundalini corridor]].
- See [[mystical|mystical traditions]] for the map as a whole.

## Tradition

Element beyond the elements; seed syllable silence (AUM). In the Shat-chakra-nirupana (1577) and its Tantric sources, the chakras are meditation supports along sushumna — visualised, not assumed to be anatomical.

## Body

Modern syntheses pair it with the pineal. The mapping is 20th-century (Leadbeater, Woodroffe), not classical.

## Symbol

Balanced: meaning, connection. Excess: spiritual bypass. Deficient: cynicism, disconnection.

## Practice

Breath, seed-sound chanting, colour visualisation, asana focused on the region; in trauma-informed work, chakras function as a somatic vocabulary for where feeling lives.
