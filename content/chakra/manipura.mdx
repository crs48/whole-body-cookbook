Will, agency, digestion of experience, personal power. "City of jewels."

[[wp:Manipura|Manipura]] is one of the seven chakras of the yogic subtle body. The atlas draws it as a disk on the midline: halfway from the navel to the xiphoid. The formula is an offset along landmarks, so the same point works on a mesh later.

## What people pin

Gut feeling and self-trust: the trust-yourself recipe points here. Digestion and the [[stomach]]. Anger and will. The [[hd-solar-plexus|HD solar plexus]] is nearby but not identical.

## In the atlas

- Family: centers, group chakras. Turning the chakras on turns the [[human-design|Human Design centers]] off; the two maps never sit at full opacity together.
- Rooms: [[solar-plexus]], [[stomach]], the [[enteric]] nervous system.
- See [[mystical|mystical traditions]] for the map as a whole.

## Tradition

Element fire; seed syllable RAM. In the Shat-chakra-nirupana (1577) and its Tantric sources, the chakras are meditation supports along sushumna — visualised, not assumed to be anatomical.

## Body

Modern syntheses pair it with the pancreas and adrenals. The mapping is 20th-century (Leadbeater, Woodroffe), not classical.

## Symbol

Balanced: confidence, warmth. Excess: domination, burnout. Deficient: shame, passivity.

## Practice

Breath, seed-sound chanting, colour visualisation, asana focused on the region; in trauma-informed work, chakras function as a somatic vocabulary for where feeling lives.
