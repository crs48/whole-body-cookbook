[[wp:Medical astrology|Medical astrology]] assigned each zodiac sign a part of the body, head to foot: Aries the [[crown|head]], Taurus the [[throat|neck]], Gemini the [[lungs]] and arms, Cancer the chest, Leo the [[heart]] and back, Virgo the [[intestines|gut]], Libra the [[kidneys]], Scorpio the [[pelvis]], Sagittarius the thighs, Capricorn the knees, Aquarius the calves, Pisces the [[feet]]. The [[wp:Zodiac Man|Zodiac Man]] woodcut is the ancestor of this atlas's plate.

## How the atlas draws it

The zodiac is a circle in the body plane centred on the origin at radius 5, visible at the Sky stop. Zero degrees Aries sits at the left and the signs run counter-clockwise, the usual natal-chart layout. Planets are placeholders in v1: there is no ephemeris yet. A natal chart is a later mode that tints which signs and planets are yours; the wheel itself stays universal.

## What people pin

Timing and transits as a way of talking about mood weather. Sun, Moon, and rising as shorthand for constitution. The folk planets on rooms: Sun on the heart, Mercury on the [[brain]], Jupiter on the [[liver]], Saturn on the [[skin]].
