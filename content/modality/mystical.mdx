Every contemplative tradition drew a body. The yogic map has seven [[wp:Chakra|chakras]] on the [[spine]] and a serpent, [[wp:Kundalini|kundalini]], that rises through them. Hesychast prayer descends into the [[heart]]. Sufism keeps its stations there too. The kabbalistic tree is laid over the whole figure. The cookbook draws the chakra map and leaves the rest as text, on the principle that they are different drawings of the same rooms.

## How the atlas draws it

- Seven chakras as disks on landmark segments: [[muladhara]], [[svadhisthana]], [[manipura]], [[anahata]], [[vishuddha]], [[ajna]], [[sahasrara]]. Anahata, for example, sits 0.55 of the way from the xiphoid to the sternal notch.
- The soul star above the [[crown]] and the earth star below the [[feet]] are the eighth and ninth beads, at plus and minus 1.28.
- The aura shells and the Merkaba are larger circles in the same body space, visible at the Subtle stop.
- Sushumna, ida, and pingala are in the channels picker alongside the meridians.
