Human Design is a synthesis from the late 1980s that lays nine centers on a body outline, connects them with 36 channels, and reads 64 gates from a wheel that is also the I Ching and the zodiac. The cookbook draws it on the person rather than as a separate poster.

## How the atlas draws it

- The nine centers are disks on landmarks: [[hd-head]], [[hd-ajna]], [[hd-throat]], [[hd-g]], [[hd-ego]], [[hd-spleen]], [[hd-solar-plexus]], [[hd-sacral]], [[hd-root]]. Turning them on turns the chakras off, so the two maps are never both at full opacity.
- The 36 channels are straight segments between centers, in the channels family.
- The 64-gate mandala is one ring at the Sky stop. The same numbers are the I Ching hexagrams and the [[gene-keys|Gene Keys]]; there is no second wheel. Gate 25 straddles zero degrees Aries and gate 41 opens the Rave year at two degrees Aquarius.
- A future your-chart mode only tints which centers, channels, and gates are defined. The geometry is universal.

## What people pin

Type, strategy, and authority as self-permission. Open centers as the places you absorb other people. The emotional wave of the [[solar-plexus]]. Sacral energy and burnout. The cookbook records these as lived vocabulary and does not adjudicate them.
