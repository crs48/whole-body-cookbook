[[wp:Homeopathy|Homeopathy]] is a two-century-old system built on the idea of like curing like in extreme dilution. Its clinical claims are contested; Wikipedia's summary of the evidence is linked below and the cookbook does not argue with it.

What the cookbook keeps is the [[wp:Repertory (homeopathy)|repertory]]: an enormous index of how sensations are described, organised by body part and modality. Worse in the morning, better from pressure, a feeling of a band around the chest. As a vocabulary for pinning what a room feels like, it has no equal.

## How the atlas draws it

It does not. Homeopathy is sensation text on regions, not tubes on the plate. Its room is the [[whole]] because constitutions are whole-body descriptions.

## What people pin

Constitutional pictures as personality shorthand. Sensation words borrowed for [[anxiety]], [[depression]], and pain threads. Remedy experiences, kept as lived experience.

## Related

[[chinese-medicine|Chinese medicine]] and [[astrology]] share the constitutional habit.
