The Gene Keys are Richard Rudd's contemplative reading of the same 64 gates that [[human-design|Human Design]] and the [[wp:I Ching|I Ching]] use. Each key has a spectrum of frequencies, and the 64 are grouped into 21 codon rings that mirror the genetic code's amino acids.

This entry records structure only: the 64 numbers and their ring groupings, which are public. It does not reproduce the book's commentary.

## How the atlas draws it

- The 64 gates are the one mandala ring at the Sky stop. Gate ids are shared: `gate.1` through `gate.64`.
- The 21 codon rings are drawn as chords between member gates on a slightly inner ring. Click a ring and its gates light on the mandala, along with the on-body [[human-design|HD channels]] those gates feed. Two rings hold a single gate: Origin (41, the start codon) and Miracles (35).
- Gene Keys are a label mode on HD channels, not a fifth geometry.

## What people pin

Profiles and life's-work keys as journal prompts. Shadow language for [[depression]] and [[anxiety]] weather. Contemplation as a slow practice that fits the slow-down recipe.
