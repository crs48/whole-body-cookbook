[[wp:Traditional Chinese medicine|Chinese medicine]] gives the cookbook its channels. Twelve regular [[wp:Meridian (Chinese medicine)|meridians]] run the length of the body, each paired with an organ and an element, and the emotions are filed by organ: anger in the [[liver]], joy and agitation in the [[heart]], worry in the [[spleen]], grief in the [[lungs]], fear in the [[kidneys]].

## How the atlas draws it

The twelve meridians are the default channel layer, painted copper through landmarks. Yin meridians are drawn on the subject's left limbs and yang on the right, for legibility; the body has all twelve on both sides. The Du and Ren vessels, two of the eight extraordinary vessels, are in the picker; the other six are data to add.

- Metal: [[lung-meridian]], [[large-intestine-meridian]]
- Earth: [[stomach-meridian]], [[spleen-meridian]]
- Fire: [[heart-meridian]], [[small-intestine-meridian]], [[pericardium-meridian]], [[triple-warmer-meridian]]
- Water: [[bladder-meridian]], [[kidney-meridian]]
- Wood: [[gallbladder-meridian]], [[liver-meridian]]

## What people pin

Acupuncture experiences. Liver qi stagnation as a name for stuck [[depression]]. Kidney deficiency as a name for the reserve that does not refill in [[cfs|ME / CFS]]. Wei qi as the [[skin]]'s field. The cookbook treats these as a coherent folk language, not as claims to be verified here.
