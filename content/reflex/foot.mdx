The whole body projected onto the soles; pressure on a zone is held to affect the corresponding organ. Systematised by Eunice Ingham (1930s) from Fitzgerald's zone therapy; older roots in Egyptian and Chinese foot work.

## Tradition

Ten longitudinal zones run from toes to head; the left foot mirrors the left side of the body. Both feet together are the body seen from behind.

## Evidence

Reviews find reflexology relaxing and modestly helpful for anxiety and pain, with no evidence that specific zones map to organs. The foot's 7,000 nerve endings and the relaxation response are the likely mechanisms.

## Practice

Thumb-walking across zones, 30–60 min sessions; self-treatment with a ball underfoot.
