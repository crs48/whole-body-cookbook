Auriculotherapy — mapped by Paul Nogier (1957) and developed in China. The ear is one of the few body surfaces innervated by the vagus (auricular branch), which is why it is taken seriously in neuromodulation research.

## Body

The concha carries vagal fibres; the rest of the ear is served by trigeminal, facial and cervical nerves — a small surface with four nerve inputs.

## Evidence

Transcutaneous auricular vagus nerve stimulation (taVNS) is an active research field for epilepsy, depression and inflammation. The NADA five-point protocol for addiction has mixed trial results. Point-organ mapping remains unproven.

## Practice

Ear seeds (vaccaria), press-needles, taVNS devices clipped to the concha.
