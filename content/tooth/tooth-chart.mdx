From biological dentistry (Voll, Kramer, 1950s–70s): incisors → Kidney/Bladder; canines → Liver/Gallbladder; premolars → Lung/Large Intestine (upper) or Stomach/Spleen (lower); molars the reverse; wisdom teeth → Heart/Small Intestine.

## Tradition

A chronic tooth or root-canal problem is read as a burden on its meridian, and vice versa.

## Evidence

Untested as a diagnostic system; oral infection does affect systemic inflammation and cardiovascular risk in general.

## Practice

Used by holistic dentists to prioritise treatment and to look for non-dental causes of tooth pain.
