One of the four pillars of Chinese diagnosis. Body colour, coating, shape and moisture read together; the map assigns regions to organs.

## Tradition

Pale = deficiency or cold; red = heat; purple = stagnation; thick coat = damp; cracks = dryness or yin deficiency; scalloped edges = Spleen qi deficiency.

## Evidence

Tongue changes accompany dehydration, anaemia, B12 deficiency and infection; AI tongue-image classification is an active research area in China, with inconsistent reproducibility of the organ map.
