Reading health from iris markings, systematised by Ignaz von Péczely (1880s) and Bernard Jensen (1950s). Widely practised in naturopathy.

## Tradition

Lacunae, radii, pigment spots and the autonomic nerve wreath are read as signs of organ weakness or toxicity in the corresponding zone.

## Evidence

Controlled studies (e.g. Ernst 2000 review) find iridology no better than chance at detecting disease. The iris pattern is stable from infancy — which is why it works for biometric identification, not for tracking health.

## Practice

Included here as a map people use; treat it as a lens, not a test.
