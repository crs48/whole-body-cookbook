Japanese abdominal palpation (fukushin), central to Masunaga's Zen Shiatsu: each organ has a diagnostic and a treatment zone on the belly, read by touch for kyo (empty) and jitsu (full).

## Tradition

The hara is also the seat of self in Japanese culture — hara-gei (belly art), the centre from which martial artists move.

## Practice

Practitioner palpates with the fingers while the client breathes; treatment follows the most kyo–jitsu pair.
