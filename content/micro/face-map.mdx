Chinese face reading: organs, constitution and character read from the face's zones, colours and lines. Adopted by skincare as "face mapping" for acne.

## Tradition

Colour diagnosis is part of classical Chinese medicine: red = heat, pale = deficiency, yellow = damp, dark = cold or Kidney. Each zone reflects the organ that feeds it.

## Evidence

Some skin changes do reflect internal disease (jaundice, butterfly rash, xanthelasma); the zone-to-organ map itself is untested.
