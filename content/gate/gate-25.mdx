I Ching: Innocence — the unexpected; act without calculation.

## Tradition

Human Design gate keynote: Spirit of the Self. Activated by a planet's position at birth or 88° before; a gate is half a channel — two gates make a defined channel and colour in two centers.

## Symbol

Gene Key 25 (Richard Rudd): shadow → gift → siddhi = Constriction → Acceptance → Universal Love. Contemplation moves the frequency from shadow toward siddhi.

## Evidence

The I Ching (c. 1000 BCE, Zhou) is a divination and wisdom text; its 64 hexagrams map to the 64 codons only by count. Human Design and Gene Keys are contemporary syntheses (1987, 2009). Keynotes above are from memory of the published systems — verify against your sources.
