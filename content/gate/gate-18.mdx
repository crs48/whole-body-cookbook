I Ching: Work on What Has Been Spoiled — repair the decayed.

## Tradition

Human Design gate keynote: Correction. Activated by a planet's position at birth or 88° before; a gate is half a channel — two gates make a defined channel and colour in two centers.

## Symbol

Gene Key 18 (Richard Rudd): shadow → gift → siddhi = Judgement → Integrity → Perfection. Contemplation moves the frequency from shadow toward siddhi.

## Evidence

The I Ching (c. 1000 BCE, Zhou) is a divination and wisdom text; its 64 hexagrams map to the 64 codons only by count. Human Design and Gene Keys are contemporary syntheses (1987, 2009). Keynotes above are from memory of the published systems — verify against your sources.
