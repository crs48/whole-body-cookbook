[[wp:Major depressive disorder|Depression]] is the recipe with the most rooms. Medicine files it in the [[brain]]; [[chinese-medicine|Chinese medicine]] files it in the [[liver]] as qi that forgot how to move; the gut-brain people file it in the [[intestines]]; and the person living it usually files it in the chest and the bed.

## What people pin

Getting up. Walking anyway. Light, sleep, and the [[spine|move-daily]] recipe. Inflammation and food. Medication threads, kept as lived experience rather than advice. The difference between sadness and the flat weather that is not sadness at all.
