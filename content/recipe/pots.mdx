[[wp:Postural orthostatic tachycardia syndrome|POTS]] is the cookbook's first recipe and the reason the [[autonomic]] field has its own room. Stand up, and the [[heart]] rate jumps; the blood pools in the [[feet]] and legs; the [[brain]] fogs.

## What people pin

Compression socks as a character trait. Salt and fluid. Lying down with legs up. Heat, showers, and standing in queues. The overlap with [[eds|EDS]], [[cfs|ME / CFS]], and [[long-covid|long COVID]]. Being told it is [[anxiety]] and knowing the difference.
