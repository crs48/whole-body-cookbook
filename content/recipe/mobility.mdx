Mobility is a lived recipe rather than a diagnosis. It covers [[wp:Mobility aid|aids]], pain, fatigue, stairs, and the [[wp:Social model of disability|social model]]: the body is not the only thing that needs adjusting.

## What people pin

A cane is a meridian you can hold. Wheelchair days and walking days in the same week. Hand pain from crutches. Where the lifts are. [[eds|EDS]] and [[pots|POTS]] as the usual neighbours. The [[spine]] and [[pelvis]] as the rooms that hurt, the [[hands]] as the rooms that carry.
