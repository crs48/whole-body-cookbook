[[wp:Ehlers–Danlos syndrome|Ehlers-Danlos]] and hypermobility spectrum disorders are connective-tissue conditions, so they touch nearly every room: the [[skin]] that stretches, the [[spine]] and [[pelvis]] that do not hold, the [[hands]] and legs that subluxate, and the [[autonomic]] field, because [[pots|POTS]] so often comes with it.

## What people pin

Party tricks that turned out to be a diagnosis. Braces, taping, and [[mobility]] aids. Gut motility. Bruising. The trifecta with POTS and mast-cell threads. Fascia as the tissue nobody mentioned. Being young and creaky.
