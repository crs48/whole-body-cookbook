[[wp:Cancer|Cancer]] is not one room. It is filed under the [[whole]] because it can start anywhere, with the [[skin]], [[lungs]], and [[intestines|gut]] as the rooms people most often mention and the [[spleen]] standing in for the immune conversation.

This entry is a map of what people talk about. It is not treatment information; oncology belongs to oncologists.

## What people pin

Treatment weather: chemo days, radiation fatigue, the [[stomach]] on the drugs. Appetite and food as morale. Hair and the [[skin]]. Being asked how you are. Survivorship and the strange quiet after. Folk maps that people find comforting and folk maps that people find insulting, both recorded as such.
