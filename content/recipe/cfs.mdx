[[wp:Myalgic encephalomyelitis/chronic fatigue syndrome|ME / CFS]] is another named audience on the product map. Its signature is post-exertional malaise: the crash that arrives a day after doing too much. The cookbook files it under the [[autonomic]] field, the [[brain]] for fog, the [[spine]] and [[kidneys]] for the deep-reserve language of Chinese medicine, and the [[whole]].

## What people pin

Pacing: spending energy like money you do not have. The crash as a weather report. Being believed. Overlap with [[pots|POTS]], [[long-covid|long COVID]], and [[eds|EDS]]. Kidney deficiency and jing as a folk reading of the reserve that does not refill. The slow-down practice, which for this audience is not optional.
