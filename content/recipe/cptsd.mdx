[[wp:Complex post-traumatic stress disorder|CPTSD]] is one of the product map's named audiences. It lives in the [[autonomic]] field first: freeze, fawn, the long brace. The [[heart]], [[solar-plexus]], and [[pelvis]] are where the feeling is stored, and the [[brain]] is where it is narrated.

## What people pin

Thaw: the strange grief of a nervous system standing down. Ninety-second feelings, if they are actually felt. Touch as regulation. Dissociation and interoception. Somatic practices, kept as lived experience. The feel-your-body and trust-yourself recipes from the v0.1 board.
