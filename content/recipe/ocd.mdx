[[wp:Obsessive–compulsive disorder|OCD]] is the [[brain]] running a loop and the [[hands]] closing it. The cookbook adds the [[skin]], because washing lives there, and the [[solar-plexus]], because the doubt underneath the loop is felt in the belly before it is thought.

## What people pin

Intrusive thoughts as weather rather than confession. Checking, counting, and the relief that does not last. Contamination and the skin. Exposure practice as lived experience. The difference between OCD and being tidy, explained for the thousandth time.
