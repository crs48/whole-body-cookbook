[[wp:Long COVID|Long COVID]] arrived in the [[lungs]] and spread through the cookbook. Breath, the standing [[heart]] rate of [[pots|POTS]], the [[autonomic]] field, and [[brain]] fog are its usual rooms.

## What people pin

Air hunger. Post-exertional crashes that look exactly like [[cfs|ME / CFS]]. New dysautonomia in people who never had it. Loss of smell in the [[face]]. Pacing borrowed from the ME community. A very new room that the old maps have not caught up with, which is part of why it is here.
