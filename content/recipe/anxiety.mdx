[[wp:Anxiety|Anxiety]] lives in the chest and the breath before it lives in the thoughts. The cookbook keeps it across the [[heart]], the [[lungs]], the [[solar-plexus]], and the [[autonomic]] field, with the [[brain]] as the narrator.

## What people pin

Breath counting: four in, six out. Cold water, weighted blankets, long exhales. The gut-level dread that lives under the ribs. Panic that looks like [[pots|POTS]] and POTS that looks like panic. Kidney water failing to cool heart fire, in the old language.
