Yang channel of the Fire element, 23 classical points, most active 9–11 pm in the organ clock.

The Triple warmer [[wp:Meridian (Chinese medicine)|meridian]] is one of the twelve regular channels of [[chinese-medicine|Chinese medicine]]. Element: **fire**. Paired organ room: [[autonomic]]. Partner channel: [[pericardium-meridian]].

## Route on the plate

From the ring finger at TW-1 up the back of the [[right-arm|arm]] over the shoulder to behind the ear and the eyebrow at TW-23. The three burners are the upper, middle, and lower torso as regulatory zones.

## What people pin

Temperature swings and regulation in [[pots|POTS]], [[cfs|ME / CFS]], and [[long-covid|long COVID]]. The cookbook files the [[autonomic]] field here in Chinese-medicine terms.

## Tradition

Regulates the three burners (chest, upper and lower abdomen) — fluid and temperature metabolism; no Western organ equivalent. Drawn dashed: partly posterior.

## Symbol

Emotion: social warmth; threat vigilance. Element Fire; paired with its yin partner across the same element.

## Evidence

Meridians have no confirmed anatomical structure; many points coincide with nerve trunks, fascial planes and vessel bundles. Acupuncture has moderate evidence for chronic pain, nausea and some headache; point-specificity is disputed.

## Practice

Trace the channel with the hand in the direction of flow; acupressure on the listed points; stretch the channel (meridian yoga, Shin Tai).
