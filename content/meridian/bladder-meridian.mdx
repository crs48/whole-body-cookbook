Yang channel of the Water element, 67 classical points, most active 3–5 pm in the organ clock.

The Bladder [[wp:Meridian (Chinese medicine)|meridian]] is one of the twelve regular channels of [[chinese-medicine|Chinese medicine]]. Element: **water**. Paired organ room: [[kidneys]]. Partner channel: [[kidney-meridian]].

## Route on the plate

From the inner eye at BL-1 over the [[crown]], down the neck and both sides of the [[spine]] with a shu point for every organ, across the sacrum, down the back of the [[right-leg|leg]] to the little toe at BL-67.

## What people pin

Back pain and [[mobility]]. The [[sympathetic]] chain runs the same corridor, which the cookbook notes without merging them. Fear held in the low back.

## Tradition

The longest channel; its back-shu points (BL13–BL28) beside the spine reflect every organ. Drawn dashed on the front plate — it lives on the back.

## Symbol

Emotion: fear, held in the back. Element Water; paired with its yin partner across the same element.

## Evidence

Meridians have no confirmed anatomical structure; many points coincide with nerve trunks, fascial planes and vessel bundles. Acupuncture has moderate evidence for chronic pain, nausea and some headache; point-specificity is disputed.

## Practice

Trace the channel with the hand in the direction of flow; acupressure on the listed points; stretch the channel (meridian yoga, Shin Tai).
