Yin channel of the Earth element, 21 classical points, most active 9–11 am in the organ clock.

The Spleen [[wp:Meridian (Chinese medicine)|meridian]] is one of the twelve regular channels of [[chinese-medicine|Chinese medicine]]. Element: **earth**. Paired organ room: [[spleen]]. Partner channel: [[stomach-meridian]].

## Route on the plate

From the big toe at SP-1 up the inside of the [[left-leg|leg]] past the knee, over the hip to the [[spleen]] and up the side of the chest. SP-6 above the ankle is where three yin leg meridians cross.

## What people pin

Overthinking loops in [[depression]] and [[autism]]. Dampness as a folk word for heaviness and fog. Blood sugar. [[eds|EDS]] flesh and tone.

## Tradition

Transforms food to qi and blood, holds blood in the vessels, governs muscles. SP6 (Sanyinjiao) crosses three yin channels.

## Symbol

Emotion: worry, obsession. Element Earth; paired with its yang partner across the same element.

## Evidence

Meridians have no confirmed anatomical structure; many points coincide with nerve trunks, fascial planes and vessel bundles. Acupuncture has moderate evidence for chronic pain, nausea and some headache; point-specificity is disputed.

## Practice

Trace the channel with the hand in the direction of flow; acupressure on the listed points; stretch the channel (meridian yoga, Shin Tai).
