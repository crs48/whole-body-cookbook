Yin channel of the Metal element, 11 classical points, most active 3–5 am in the organ clock.

The Lung [[wp:Meridian (Chinese medicine)|meridian]] is one of the twelve regular channels of [[chinese-medicine|Chinese medicine]]. Element: **metal**. Paired organ room: [[lungs]]. Partner channel: [[large-intestine-meridian]].

## Route on the plate

From Lung-1 below the collarbone, down the inside of the [[left-arm|arm]] past the elbow and wrist to the thumb at Lung-11. The atlas draws it on the subject's left arm in lane one, beside the [[pericardium-meridian]] and [[heart-meridian]].

## What people pin

Breathing practice for [[anxiety]]. Grief that sits in the chest. The [[skin]] as the lung's territory. [[long-covid|Long COVID]] as a lung-metal story.

## Tradition

Governs qi and respiration, disperses wei qi to the skin, regulates water passages. Signs: cough, shortness of breath, weak voice, frequent colds, skin dryness.

## Symbol

Emotion: grief, letting go. Element Metal; paired with its yang partner across the same element.

## Evidence

Meridians have no confirmed anatomical structure; many points coincide with nerve trunks, fascial planes and vessel bundles. Acupuncture has moderate evidence for chronic pain, nausea and some headache; point-specificity is disputed.

## Practice

Trace the channel with the hand in the direction of flow; acupressure on the listed points; stretch the channel (meridian yoga, Shin Tai).
