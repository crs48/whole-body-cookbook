Yin channel of the Fire element, 9 classical points, most active 11 am–1 pm in the organ clock.

The Heart [[wp:Meridian (Chinese medicine)|meridian]] is one of the twelve regular channels of [[chinese-medicine|Chinese medicine]]. Element: **fire**. Paired organ room: [[heart]]. Partner channel: [[small-intestine-meridian]].

## Route on the plate

From the [[heart]] out through the armpit and down the inside of the [[left-arm|arm]] to the little finger at HT-9. HT-7 at the wrist crease is the calming point in every [[anxiety]] thread. Drawn in lane minus one beside the [[pericardium-meridian]].

## What people pin

Palpitations and [[pots|POTS]]. Insomnia. The heart as the seat of shen, the spirit. Grief and joy in the same room.

## Tradition

Governs blood and vessels and houses shen. HT7 (Shenmen, "spirit gate") at the wrist calms the mind and helps sleep.

## Symbol

Emotion: joy — and its excess, agitation. Element Fire; paired with its yang partner across the same element.

## Evidence

Meridians have no confirmed anatomical structure; many points coincide with nerve trunks, fascial planes and vessel bundles. Acupuncture has moderate evidence for chronic pain, nausea and some headache; point-specificity is disputed.

## Practice

Trace the channel with the hand in the direction of flow; acupressure on the listed points; stretch the channel (meridian yoga, Shin Tai).
