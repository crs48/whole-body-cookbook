Yang channel of the Fire element, 19 classical points, most active 1–3 pm in the organ clock.

The Small intestine [[wp:Meridian (Chinese medicine)|meridian]] is one of the twelve regular channels of [[chinese-medicine|Chinese medicine]]. Element: **fire**. Paired organ room: [[intestines]]. Partner channel: [[heart-meridian]].

## Route on the plate

From the little finger at SI-1 up the back of the [[right-arm|arm]] over the shoulder blade to the neck and the ear at SI-19, crossing the upper [[spine]].

## What people pin

Shoulder and neck pain. Sorting: the folk job of this organ is deciding what to absorb, which people apply to information as readily as food.

## Tradition

Separates pure from impure — in food and in judgement. Shoulder and neck pain are its territory.

## Symbol

Emotion: discernment; clarity vs confusion. Element Fire; paired with its yin partner across the same element.

## Evidence

Meridians have no confirmed anatomical structure; many points coincide with nerve trunks, fascial planes and vessel bundles. Acupuncture has moderate evidence for chronic pain, nausea and some headache; point-specificity is disputed.

## Practice

Trace the channel with the hand in the direction of flow; acupressure on the listed points; stretch the channel (meridian yoga, Shin Tai).
