Yang channel of the Wood element, 44 classical points, most active 11 pm–1 am in the organ clock.

The Gallbladder [[wp:Meridian (Chinese medicine)|meridian]] is one of the twelve regular channels of [[chinese-medicine|Chinese medicine]]. Element: **wood**. Paired organ room: [[liver]]. Partner channel: [[liver-meridian]].

## Route on the plate

From the outer eye at GB-1, zigzagging over the side of the head, down the neck and shoulder at GB-21, down the side of the ribs and hip, the outside of the [[right-leg|leg]] to the fourth toe at GB-44.

## What people pin

Migraine and temple pain. Shoulder knots at GB-21. Decision fatigue in [[adhd|ADHD]]. Hip and side-body [[mobility]] threads.

## Tradition

Governs decision-making and the sides of the body; GB20 at the skull base and GB34 below the knee are workhorse points.

## Symbol

Emotion: indecision, resentment. Element Wood; paired with its yin partner across the same element.

## Evidence

Meridians have no confirmed anatomical structure; many points coincide with nerve trunks, fascial planes and vessel bundles. Acupuncture has moderate evidence for chronic pain, nausea and some headache; point-specificity is disputed.

## Practice

Trace the channel with the hand in the direction of flow; acupressure on the listed points; stretch the channel (meridian yoga, Shin Tai).
