Yang channel of the Earth element, 45 classical points, most active 7–9 am in the organ clock.

The Stomach [[wp:Meridian (Chinese medicine)|meridian]] is one of the twelve regular channels of [[chinese-medicine|Chinese medicine]]. Element: **earth**. Paired organ room: [[stomach]]. Partner channel: [[spleen-meridian]].

## Route on the plate

From under the eye at ST-1, down the jaw and [[throat]], across the chest and belly past the [[stomach]], down the front of the [[right-leg|leg]] to the second toe. ST-36 below the knee is the tonifying point people mention most.

## What people pin

Nausea and appetite with [[anxiety]] and [[adhd|ADHD]] medication. The [[intestines|inflammatory-food]] conversation. Worry as earth weather.

## Tradition

Receives and ripens food; the front-of-body channel. ST36 (Zusanli) is the great tonifying point for energy and digestion.

## Symbol

Emotion: worry, overthinking. Element Earth; paired with its yin partner across the same element.

## Evidence

Meridians have no confirmed anatomical structure; many points coincide with nerve trunks, fascial planes and vessel bundles. Acupuncture has moderate evidence for chronic pain, nausea and some headache; point-specificity is disputed.

## Practice

Trace the channel with the hand in the direction of flow; acupressure on the listed points; stretch the channel (meridian yoga, Shin Tai).
