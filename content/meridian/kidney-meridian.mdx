Yin channel of the Water element, 27 classical points, most active 5–7 pm in the organ clock.

The Kidney [[wp:Meridian (Chinese medicine)|meridian]] is one of the twelve regular channels of [[chinese-medicine|Chinese medicine]]. Element: **water**. Paired organ room: [[kidneys]]. Partner channel: [[bladder-meridian]].

## Route on the plate

From Kidney-1, the bubbling spring on the sole of the [[feet|foot]], around the inner ankle and up the inside of the [[left-leg|leg]], past the pubic bone and up the front of the belly and chest beside the midline to the collarbone.

## What people pin

Deep fatigue in [[cfs|ME / CFS]]. Salt and fluid in [[pots|POTS]]. Fear in [[cptsd|CPTSD]]. Grounding practices that start at the sole.

## Tradition

Stores essence, roots yin and yang, governs bones, ears and reproduction. KI1 draws energy down; KI3 tonifies.

## Symbol

Emotion: fear, will. Element Water; paired with its yang partner across the same element.

## Evidence

Meridians have no confirmed anatomical structure; many points coincide with nerve trunks, fascial planes and vessel bundles. Acupuncture has moderate evidence for chronic pain, nausea and some headache; point-specificity is disputed.

## Practice

Trace the channel with the hand in the direction of flow; acupressure on the listed points; stretch the channel (meridian yoga, Shin Tai).
