Yin channel of the Fire element, 9 classical points, most active 7–9 pm in the organ clock.

The Pericardium [[wp:Meridian (Chinese medicine)|meridian]] is one of the twelve regular channels of [[chinese-medicine|Chinese medicine]]. Element: **fire**. Paired organ room: [[heart]]. Partner channel: [[triple-warmer-meridian]].

## Route on the plate

From beside the nipple at PC-1, down the centre of the inside of the [[left-arm|arm]] to the middle finger at PC-9. PC-6 above the wrist is the nausea point on every travel band. Drawn in lane zero between the [[lung-meridian]] and [[heart-meridian]].

## What people pin

Nausea. Chest tightness in [[anxiety]]. The heart's minister: the room where protection and armour live, which [[cptsd|CPTSD]] threads recognise.

## Tradition

The Heart's protector and the channel of emotional intimacy; PC6 (Neiguan) is the nausea and anxiety point.

## Symbol

Emotion: protection, vulnerability. Element Fire; paired with its yang partner across the same element.

## Evidence

Meridians have no confirmed anatomical structure; many points coincide with nerve trunks, fascial planes and vessel bundles. Acupuncture has moderate evidence for chronic pain, nausea and some headache; point-specificity is disputed.

## Practice

Trace the channel with the hand in the direction of flow; acupressure on the listed points; stretch the channel (meridian yoga, Shin Tai).
