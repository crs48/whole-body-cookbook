The [[wp:Sympathetic trunk|sympathetic trunk]] is a pair of ganglion chains running either side of the [[spine]] from the neck to the sacrum. It raises heart rate, tightens vessels, opens airways, and gets the body upright. The atlas draws both chains as mirrored dashed lines in lane two beside the vertebral landmarks.

## What people pin

The standing problem: [[pots|POTS]] is partly this system overcorrecting. Hyperarousal and the long brace in [[cptsd|CPTSD]]. [[anxiety]] as a system doing its job too well. The slow-down practice from the v0.1 board is largely about letting this chain stand down.

## In the atlas

- Family: channels, group nerves.
- Its partner and opponent is the [[vagus]]; together they are the [[autonomic]] field.
- The [[bladder-meridian]] and the Du vessel share its corridor without being it.
