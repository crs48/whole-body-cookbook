The body's widest nerve (\~2 cm). Sciatica is its irritation — a disc, the piriformis, or the sacroiliac joint.

The [[wp:Sciatic nerve|sciatic nerve]] leaves the [[pelvis]] and runs down the back of the thigh, splitting at the knee to reach the [[feet]]. The atlas draws it mirrored on both legs: sacrum, trochanter, knee, heel.

## What people pin

[[wp:Sciatica|Sciatica]]: the burn down one leg. Sitting, standing, and the [[mobility]] conversation. Hip instability in [[eds|EDS]]. Walking as the recipe that both hurts and helps.

## In the atlas

- Family: channels, group nerves.
- Shares its corridor with the [[bladder-meridian]] down the back of the leg.

## Body

Motor to the hamstrings and everything below the knee; sensory to the back and side of the leg and the foot.

## Tradition

Follows the Bladder meridian almost exactly; BL40 (Weizhong) behind the knee and GB30 (Huantiao) at the hip are its acupuncture treatment points.
