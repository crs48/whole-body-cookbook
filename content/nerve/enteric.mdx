The [[wp:Enteric nervous system|enteric nervous system]] is a web of several hundred million neurons lining the gut from the [[stomach]] to the rectum. It can run digestion without the brain and reports upward through the [[vagus]]. The atlas draws it as a closed dashed loop around the [[intestines]].

## What people pin

Gut feeling as literal. Motility in [[eds|EDS]] and [[autism]]. The microbiome and mood in [[depression]]. Interoception, or the lack of it. The [[solar-plexus]] as the place a decision is felt.

- Family: channels, group nerves.
- The third branch of the [[autonomic]] field, with the [[vagus]] and the [[sympathetic]] chain.
