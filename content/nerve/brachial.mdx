The nerve network of the arm: median, ulnar, radial, musculocutaneous and axillary nerves. Neck and shoulder posture compresses it — thoracic outlet syndrome.

## Body

Motor and sensory to the whole upper limb; injuries at birth (Erb's palsy) or from traction.

## Tradition

The six arm meridians pass through this territory; the scalene and pectoral zones are dense with points.
