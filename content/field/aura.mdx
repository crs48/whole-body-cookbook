The luminous envelope described by clairvoyants (Leadbeater, Brennan) and by traditions as wei qi (Chinese), pranamaya kosha (yoga), or the astral body. Modern energy medicine calls it the biofield.

## Tradition

Brennan's Hands of Light describes seven layers alternating structured and fluid, each linked to a chakra; Theosophy gives etheric, astral, mental, causal. Chinese medicine: wei qi circulates at the surface and just beyond, warming and defending.

## Body

The body does have measurable fields: the heart's magnetic field (\~1 pT at the chest, detectable by SQUID magnetometers to \~1 m), infrared radiation, and a bioelectric skin potential. None matches the described layers.

## Evidence

Kirlian photography images skin moisture and corona discharge, not an aura. Emily Rosa's 1998 test of therapeutic-touch practitioners found no ability to detect a field. Synesthetic "aura seeing" is a documented perceptual phenomenon.

## Practice

Energy healing (Reiki, therapeutic touch, pranic healing) works in this layer; the aura is also a useful metaphor for personal space and sensory boundary.
