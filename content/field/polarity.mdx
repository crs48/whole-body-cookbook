Almost every system divides the body into a receptive and an active half, and the sides mostly agree: left–lunar–feminine–yin, right–solar–masculine–yang.

## Tradition

Yoga: ida (left, lunar, cooling) and pingala (right, solar, heating) coil around sushumna; nadi shodhana balances them. Chinese medicine: left is yang and right yin in some diagnostic conventions (pulse positions differ left and right). Kabbalah: the right pillar is Mercy, the left Severity — as the Tree faces you, so mirrored on the body. Alchemy: Sol and Luna, the rebis. Merkaba: upper tetrahedron masculine, lower feminine.

## Body

Brain lateralisation is real but modest — language mostly left, spatial attention mostly right; the "logical left / creative right" personality split is a myth. The body itself is asymmetric: heart left, liver right, and the two vagal branches serve different organs.

## Evidence

Alternate-nostril breathing shows small measurable effects on autonomic balance; the nasal cycle (nostrils alternating dominance every 1–4 h) is a real physiological rhythm that the yogic account noticed early.

## Practice

Nadi shodhana, cross-lateral movement (Brain Gym, crawling patterns), attending to which side symptoms favour.
