From Hebrew merkavah, "chariot" — Ezekiel's vision and the earliest Jewish mystical tradition (Hekhalot literature, 1st–7th c.). Drunvalo Melchizedek's Flower of Life (1990s) reframed it as a counter-rotating light-body vehicle: Mer (light), Ka (spirit), Ba (body).

## Tradition

Melchizedek: the up-tetrahedron is masculine/electric, the down feminine/magnetic; the 17-breath meditation spins them in opposite directions, generating a 55-foot disc at the base of the spine. Jewish merkavah mysticism is about ascent through the heavenly palaces, not a personal geometry.

## Symbol

The star tetrahedron is the 3D form of the hexagram / Star of David / Shatkona: fire and water triangles, Shiva and Shakti, above and below united.

## Evidence

A geometric meditation image; no physical field is implied or measured.

## Practice

The Mer-Ka-Ba meditation (17 breaths, mudras, visualisation) — a structured breath and imagery practice.
