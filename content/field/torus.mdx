The doughnut-shaped flow that a spinning dipole makes — the shape of the earth's magnetosphere, a magnet's field lines, a smoke ring. Energy teachings picture the human field the same way, with the heart as its centre.

## Tradition

HeartMath and Drunvalo Melchizedek popularised the human torus; in these accounts it is the heart's electromagnetic field extended and organised. The chakras become vortices on its axis; the aura its outer boundary. Many traditions describe the same shape without the word: the microcosmic orbit, the fountain breath.

## Body

The heart's field is a dipole, so it really does have a toroidal shape close to the chest — at picotesla strength, far below the earth's field. The "fountain" motion also describes cerebrospinal fluid and lymph return in a loose sense.

## Evidence

Field strength, not shape, is the issue: the heart's field is measurable but tiny, and there is no evidence of the large, coherent torus described in energy teachings.

## Practice

Fountain / torus breathing: inhale up the spine, exhale out the crown and down around the body back to the feet; used in qigong, HeartMath coherence and many meditation systems.
