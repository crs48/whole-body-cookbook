The grounding chakra of the transpersonal set: the body's anchor into the planet, ancestral lineage and the collective. A New Age addition, widespread in energy healing since the 1990s.

## Tradition

Paired with the soul star; described as brown, black or magma-coloured; connects to the ley grid and Gaia.

## Practice

Grounding cords, barefoot practice, visualising roots.
