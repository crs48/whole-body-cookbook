The "elixir field" of Taoist internal alchemy and qigong — the reservoir where qi is gathered and refined; in Japanese arts the hara or seika tanden, the physical and psychological centre of gravity.

## Tradition

Three dantien: lower (jing/essence, below the navel), middle (qi, the heart), upper (shen, the brow). Qigong sinks the breath and attention here; internal alchemy circulates from here.

## Body

Coincides with the body's centre of mass in standing and with the pelvic floor–diaphragm pressure system — which is why "moving from the hara" works.

## Practice

Abdominal breathing, standing meditation (zhan zhuang), tai chi, aikido.
