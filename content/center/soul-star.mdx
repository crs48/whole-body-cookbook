A New Age extension of the seven: the seat of the soul's records, karmic patterns and the connection to the higher self. Not in classical Tantric sources.

## Tradition

Often placed with the earth star as a pair bracketing the body — soul above, earth below — and used as the anchor points of the torus and Merkaba.

## Practice

Visualised as a white or gold star in meditation; opened after the seven are balanced.
