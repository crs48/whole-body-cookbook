Love, pleasure, harmony; rules Taurus and Libra.

## Tradition

Rules the throat, kidneys, veins and skin's beauty; warm and moist. Venus placement describes how one relates and what one enjoys.

## Body

Oxytocin, the parasympathetic "rest and digest" state.

## Evidence

Medical astrology is a historical framework (Hippocratic humours → Galen → medieval zodiac man); it shaped Western medicine until the 17th century and has no predictive validity in modern trials.
