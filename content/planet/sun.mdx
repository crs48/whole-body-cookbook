The self, vitality, the heart; rules Leo.

## Tradition

Rules the heart, spine and general vitality; hot and dry. The Sun sign is the core identity in modern astrology; in Human Design the Sun carries 70 % of the imprint.

## Body

Circadian rhythm is literally solar: light sets cortisol, melatonin and body temperature.

## Evidence

Medical astrology is a historical framework (Hippocratic humours → Galen → medieval zodiac man); it shaped Western medicine until the 17th century and has no predictive validity in modern trials.
