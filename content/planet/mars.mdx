Drive, aggression, heat; rules Aries and Scorpio.

## Tradition

Rules the head, face, blood, muscles and genitals; hot and dry; inflammation, fever, cuts and surgery.

## Body

Adrenaline, testosterone, the sympathetic surge.

## Evidence

Medical astrology is a historical framework (Hippocratic humours → Galen → medieval zodiac man); it shaped Western medicine until the 17th century and has no predictive validity in modern trials.
