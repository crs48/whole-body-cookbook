Mind, speech, nerves, exchange; rules Gemini and Virgo.

## Tradition

Rules the lungs, hands, nervous system and intestines; the messenger between body systems.

## Body

Nerve conduction, breath and the gut–brain axis are its modern echoes.

## Evidence

Medical astrology is a historical framework (Hippocratic humours → Galen → medieval zodiac man); it shaped Western medicine until the 17th century and has no predictive validity in modern trials.
