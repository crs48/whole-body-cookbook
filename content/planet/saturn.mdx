Structure, limit, time; rules Capricorn and Aquarius.

## Tradition

Rules bones, teeth, skin, joints, knees and the spine; cold and dry; chronic and slow conditions. The Saturn return (\~29.5 years) is a life-stage marker.

## Body

Collagen, bone density, the aging clock.

## Evidence

Medical astrology is a historical framework (Hippocratic humours → Galen → medieval zodiac man); it shaped Western medicine until the 17th century and has no predictive validity in modern trials.
