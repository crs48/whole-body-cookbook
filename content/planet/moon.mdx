Emotion, habit, the body's tides; rules Cancer.

## Tradition

Rules the stomach, breasts, womb and fluids; cold and moist. Moon sign = emotional nature; the lunar cycle was the ancient clock of menstruation and madness ("lunacy").

## Body

The 29.5-day lunar month approximates the menstrual cycle; studies find weak or no lunar effects on sleep, births or mood.

## Evidence

Medical astrology is a historical framework (Hippocratic humours → Galen → medieval zodiac man); it shaped Western medicine until the 17th century and has no predictive validity in modern trials.
