Expansion, growth, faith; rules Sagittarius and Pisces.

## Tradition

Rules the liver, thighs, fat and growth; warm and moist; the "greater benefic".

## Body

Metabolic expansion — the liver's synthesis and storage.

## Evidence

Medical astrology is a historical framework (Hippocratic humours → Galen → medieval zodiac man); it shaped Western medicine until the 17th century and has no predictive validity in modern trials.
