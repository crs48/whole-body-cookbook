Willpower, ego, material world, promises. A motor. Gates 21, 40, 26, 51.

The **Heart / ego center** is one of the nine centers of [[human-design|Human Design]]. The atlas draws it as a verdigris disk beside the xiphoid on the subject's left, with the physical heart. Gates: 21, 40, 26, 51.

## What people pin

Willpower and its limits. Promises and burnout. Worth. The cookbook places it with the [[heart]] rather than as a separate poster.

## In the atlas

- Family: centers, group Human Design. Turning the HD centers on turns the chakras off.
- Channels from this center are straight segments in the channels picker; the [[gene-keys|Gene Keys]] and I Ching names are a label mode on those channels, not a separate geometry.
- Rooms: [[heart]], [[solar-plexus]].

## Tradition

Defined (coloured in a chart): reliable willpower; can make and keep promises. Open / undefined: nothing to prove; inconsistent will; learns not to over-promise.

## Body

Associated with heart, stomach, gallbladder and thymus in HD teaching.

## Evidence

Human Design (Ra Uru Hu, 1987) synthesises the I Ching, astrology, Kabbalah and the chakras with a neutrino-based transmission story; it has no scientific standing and is best read as a symbolic map, like a tarot spread of the body.

## Practice

Charts are cast from birth time; the map is used for decision-making style (Authority), energy type and where one is conditioned by others.
