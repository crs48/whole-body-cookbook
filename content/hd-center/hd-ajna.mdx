Awareness center of the mind: conceptualising, opinions, certainty. Gates 47, 24, 4, 17, 43, 11.

The **Ajna center** is one of the nine centers of [[human-design|Human Design]]. The atlas draws it as a verdigris disk at the glabella. Gates: 47, 24, 4, 17, 11, 43.

## What people pin

Opinions, concepts, and the anxiety of not knowing. [[adhd|ADHD]] and [[autism|autistic]] threads about how a mind samples the world. Shares the landmark with [[ajna]].

## In the atlas

- Family: centers, group Human Design. Turning the HD centers on turns the chakras off.
- Channels from this center are straight segments in the channels picker; the [[gene-keys|Gene Keys]] and I Ching names are a label mode on those channels, not a separate geometry.
- Rooms: [[brain]], [[face]].

## Tradition

Defined (coloured in a chart): a fixed way of processing; mental certainty. Open / undefined: flexible, open-minded, can pretend to be certain.

## Body

Named after the brow chakra but framed as cognition rather than intuition.

## Evidence

Human Design (Ra Uru Hu, 1987) synthesises the I Ching, astrology, Kabbalah and the chakras with a neutrino-based transmission story; it has no scientific standing and is best read as a symbolic map, like a tarot spread of the body.

## Practice

Charts are cast from birth time; the map is used for decision-making style (Authority), energy type and where one is conditioned by others.
