Identity, direction, love and the "magnetic monopole" that holds the self together. Gates 1, 13, 25, 46, 2, 15, 10, 7.

The **G / identity center** is one of the nine centers of [[human-design|Human Design]]. The atlas draws it as a verdigris disk halfway from the xiphoid to the sternal notch. Gates: 7, 1, 13, 10, 25, 15, 46, 2.

## What people pin

Direction and belonging. Who you are when the other centers are quiet. Sits with the [[heart]] and near [[anahata]].

## In the atlas

- Family: centers, group Human Design. Turning the HD centers on turns the chakras off.
- Channels from this center are straight segments in the channels picker; the [[gene-keys|Gene Keys]] and I Ching names are a label mode on those channels, not a separate geometry.
- Rooms: [[heart]], [[lungs]].

## Tradition

Defined (coloured in a chart): fixed sense of self and direction. Open / undefined: fluid identity; finds self through people and place; sensitive to environment.

## Body

Sits at the heart's height; HD separates identity (G) from will (Heart/Ego).

## Evidence

Human Design (Ra Uru Hu, 1987) synthesises the I Ching, astrology, Kabbalah and the chakras with a neutrino-based transmission story; it has no scientific standing and is best read as a symbolic map, like a tarot spread of the body.

## Practice

Charts are cast from birth time; the map is used for decision-making style (Authority), energy type and where one is conditioned by others.
