Awareness of the body in the now: intuition, immune system, fear, survival instinct. Gates 48, 57, 44, 50, 32, 28, 18.

The **Spleen center** is one of the nine centers of [[human-design|Human Design]]. The atlas draws it as a verdigris disk on the subject's right below the ribs, viewer's left as in the bodygraph. Gates: 48, 57, 44, 50, 32, 28, 18.

## What people pin

Instinct in the moment. Health and immune timing. Fear as information. The cookbook files the [[autonomic]] field's instinct half here and notes the physical [[spleen]] is on the other side.

## In the atlas

- Family: centers, group Human Design. Turning the HD centers on turns the chakras off.
- Channels from this center are straight segments in the channels picker; the [[gene-keys|Gene Keys]] and I Ching names are a label mode on those channels, not a separate geometry.
- Rooms: [[spleen]], [[autonomic]], [[skin]].

## Tradition

Defined (coloured in a chart): consistent intuitive and immune awareness; spontaneous. Open / undefined: holds on to what is not good for it; sensitive to others' health and fears.

## Body

HD ties it to spleen, lymph, immune system — the body's alarm.

## Evidence

Human Design (Ra Uru Hu, 1987) synthesises the I Ching, astrology, Kabbalah and the chakras with a neutrino-based transmission story; it has no scientific standing and is best read as a symbolic map, like a tarot spread of the body.

## Practice

Charts are cast from birth time; the map is used for decision-making style (Authority), energy type and where one is conditioned by others.
