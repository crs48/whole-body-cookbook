Life-force and sexual energy, work capacity, the guttural yes/no response. Generators are defined here. Gates 5, 14, 29, 59, 9, 3, 42, 27, 34.

The **Sacral center** is one of the nine centers of [[human-design|Human Design]]. The atlas draws it as a verdigris disk halfway from the pubic symphysis to the navel. Gates: 5, 14, 29, 59, 9, 3, 42, 27, 34.

## What people pin

Energy and burnout. Response versus initiation. Sacral energy as a lived vocabulary in [[cfs|ME / CFS]] and [[depression]] threads. Shares the room with [[svadhisthana]].

## In the atlas

- Family: centers, group Human Design. Turning the HD centers on turns the chakras off.
- Channels from this center are straight segments in the channels picker; the [[gene-keys|Gene Keys]] and I Ching names are a label mode on those channels, not a separate geometry.
- Rooms: [[pelvis]], [[intestines]].

## Tradition

Defined (coloured in a chart): sustainable energy for work; responds with sounds. Open / undefined: no consistent energy; must rest more; learns when enough is enough.

## Body

Reproductive organs; overlaps svadhisthana.

## Evidence

Human Design (Ra Uru Hu, 1987) synthesises the I Ching, astrology, Kabbalah and the chakras with a neutrino-based transmission story; it has no scientific standing and is best read as a symbolic map, like a tarot spread of the body.

## Practice

Charts are cast from birth time; the map is used for decision-making style (Authority), energy type and where one is conditioned by others.
