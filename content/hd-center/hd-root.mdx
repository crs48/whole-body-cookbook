Pressure center: adrenaline, stress, the drive to act and finish. Gates 53, 60, 52, 19, 39, 41, 58, 38, 54.

The **Root center** is one of the nine centers of [[human-design|Human Design]]. The atlas draws it as a verdigris disk just below the perineum. Gates: 53, 60, 52, 19, 39, 41, 58, 38, 54.

## What people pin

Stress and the pressure to begin. Adrenaline in [[pots|POTS]] and [[cptsd|CPTSD]] threads. The base of the [[spine]]. Shares the room with [[muladhara]].

## In the atlas

- Family: centers, group Human Design. Turning the HD centers on turns the chakras off.
- Channels from this center are straight segments in the channels picker; the [[gene-keys|Gene Keys]] and I Ching names are a label mode on those channels, not a separate geometry.
- Rooms: [[pelvis]], [[spine]], [[kidneys]].

## Tradition

Defined (coloured in a chart): a fixed way of handling pressure. Open / undefined: hurries to be free of pressure; learns that pressure is not urgency.

## Body

Adrenals; overlaps muladhara.

## Evidence

Human Design (Ra Uru Hu, 1987) synthesises the I Ching, astrology, Kabbalah and the chakras with a neutrino-based transmission story; it has no scientific standing and is best read as a symbolic map, like a tarot spread of the body.

## Practice

Charts are cast from birth time; the map is used for decision-making style (Authority), energy type and where one is conditioned by others.
