Pressure center: the pressure to think, question and be inspired. Gates 64, 61, 63.

The **Head center** is one of the nine centers of [[human-design|Human Design]]. The atlas draws it as a verdigris disk 0.55 of the way from the glabella to the crown. Gates: 64, 61, 63.

## What people pin

The pressure to answer questions that are not yours. Wondering as a bodily sensation at the [[crown]]. Overlaps [[sahasrara]] and the top of the [[brain]].

## In the atlas

- Family: centers, group Human Design. Turning the HD centers on turns the chakras off.
- Channels from this center are straight segments in the channels picker; the [[gene-keys|Gene Keys]] and I Ching names are a label mode on those channels, not a separate geometry.
- Rooms: [[crown]], [[brain]].

## Tradition

Defined (coloured in a chart): consistent mental pressure and inspiration. Open / undefined: takes on others' questions; learns to enjoy inspiration without needing to resolve it.

## Body

Corresponds loosely to the crown; HD treats it as pressure, not awareness.

## Evidence

Human Design (Ra Uru Hu, 1987) synthesises the I Ching, astrology, Kabbalah and the chakras with a neutrino-based transmission story; it has no scientific standing and is best read as a symbolic map, like a tarot spread of the body.

## Practice

Charts are cast from birth time; the map is used for decision-making style (Authority), energy type and where one is conditioned by others.
