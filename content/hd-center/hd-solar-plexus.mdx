Emotional awareness in waves — hope to pain and back; the emotional Authority when defined. Gates 6, 37, 22, 36, 30, 55, 49.

The **Solar plexus center** is one of the nine centers of [[human-design|Human Design]]. The atlas draws it as a verdigris disk on the subject's left below the ribs. Gates: 6, 37, 22, 36, 30, 55, 49.

## What people pin

The emotional wave: waiting out a high or low before deciding. [[anxiety]] and [[cptsd|CPTSD]] as weather. Overlaps [[manipura]] and the [[enteric]] gut-brain.

## In the atlas

- Family: centers, group Human Design. Turning the HD centers on turns the chakras off.
- Channels from this center are straight segments in the channels picker; the [[gene-keys|Gene Keys]] and I Ching names are a label mode on those channels, not a separate geometry.
- Rooms: [[solar-plexus]], [[stomach]], [[heart]].

## Tradition

Defined (coloured in a chart): an emotional wave; clarity comes over time, never in the moment. Open / undefined: amplifies others' emotions; avoids confrontation.

## Body

Kidneys, pancreas, nervous system in HD; the celiac plexus anatomically.

## Evidence

Human Design (Ra Uru Hu, 1987) synthesises the I Ching, astrology, Kabbalah and the chakras with a neutrino-based transmission story; it has no scientific standing and is best read as a symbolic map, like a tarot spread of the body.

## Practice

Charts are cast from birth time; the map is used for decision-making style (Authority), energy type and where one is conditioned by others.
