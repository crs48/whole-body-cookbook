The manifestation center: everything is expressed through it as speech or action. 11 gates — the most connected center.

The **Throat center** is one of the nine centers of [[human-design|Human Design]]. The atlas draws it as a verdigris disk at the larynx. Gates: 62, 23, 56, 35, 12, 45, 16, 20, 31, 8, 33.

## What people pin

Speaking and doing. Voice under stress. The [[throat]] as the busiest crossroads in the graph. Shares the landmark with [[vishuddha]].

## In the atlas

- Family: centers, group Human Design. Turning the HD centers on turns the chakras off.
- Channels from this center are straight segments in the channels picker; the [[gene-keys|Gene Keys]] and I Ching names are a label mode on those channels, not a separate geometry.
- Rooms: [[throat]], the [[vagus]].

## Tradition

Defined (coloured in a chart): a consistent voice and way of acting. Open / undefined: pressure to attract attention by speaking; learns to wait for invitation.

## Body

The larynx; the point where inner process becomes outer act.

## Evidence

Human Design (Ra Uru Hu, 1987) synthesises the I Ching, astrology, Kabbalah and the chakras with a neutrino-based transmission story; it has no scientific standing and is best read as a symbolic map, like a tarot spread of the body.

## Practice

Charts are cast from birth time; the map is used for decision-making style (Authority), energy type and where one is conditioned by others.
