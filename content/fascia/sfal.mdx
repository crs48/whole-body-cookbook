Pulling and grasping; shortens with desk posture.

## Body

Anatomy Trains (Thomas Myers) traces continuous myofascial connections through dissection — muscle to tendon to bone to the next muscle in line, so tension travels the whole track.

## Tradition

Rhymes with the Pericardium / Heart / Lung channel — the same body line found by a different method.

## Evidence

Cadaver studies (Wilke et al., 2016) support continuity for the superficial back line, back functional line and front functional line; weaker for the lateral and spiral lines.

## Practice

Stretch or release along the whole line rather than the sore spot; foam rolling, structural integration, yoga sequences built line by line.
